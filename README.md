## About

Biochemist and research supervisor with over two decades of experience across industry R&D, government research institutes and universities in India, South Korea and Malaysia. My work focuses on **phytochemical bioactivity, in silico drug discovery (molecular docking, ADMET), cell culture, nanoparticles, and natural-product-based therapeutics**, with an active patent portfolio spanning cosmetics, food and medical devices.
